LocTEK is a group project built for the Locteck China Modular Furniture Design Competition, where it placed 2nd out of more than 50 teams. I led the team's end-to-end development: owning the user research, running the physical testing and coordinating the group from concept through to final submission. This page covers the whole process, but the chair itself is the product of a team effort, not mine alone.

01 · The Problem

For anyone with reduced mobility, stiff joints or poor balance, an ordinary chair turns a simple task into a real risk. Standard seating gives little support for the specific motion of lowering down or pushing back up, so people end up bracing on furniture that was never built to take that kind of weight, or avoiding certain chairs altogether. Left unaddressed, that fear of falling keeps people from moving confidently around their own home, and accelerates the loss of independence a modular, supportive chair could otherwise help preserve.

The result is a chair that gives people with reduced mobility real support for the moment ordinary furniture leaves them exposed: sitting down and standing back up.
