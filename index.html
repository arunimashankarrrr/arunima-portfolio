NiRA

Designed for anyone who wants to eat well but doesn't have the time, energy or physical ability to cook from scratch every day.

Inclusive Design

Halo Arc

Most bone-conduction headsets are built around a narrow idea of fit, overlooking afro hair, sweat and head shapes outside a narrow average. Halo Arc is built around a pull-to-slide mechanism that adjusts smoothly across the 5th to 95th percentile of head sizes, so it sits comfortably regardless of hair type or head shape.

Sustainable Design

The Wave Speaker

Most speakers are built to be thrown away: glued together from virgin materials that are near-impossible to separate at end of life. The Wave Speaker's nautilus-shaped shell is built the opposite way, from recycled aluminium and recycled polypropylene held together without a single drop of glue or solder, cutting its embodied carbon by up to 97% without giving up on sound or form.
